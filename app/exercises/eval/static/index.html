Reading the numbers
  • Match — share of checkable questions whose expected answer appears in the response. Objective, but only for short facts.
  • Grade — 1–5 from an LLM grader against a reference or rubric. Handles open answers, but the grader is a model too: it can favour longer answers or its own provider's style. Read the reasons, and prefer a grader that isn't also a candidate.
  • Latency / calls / tokens — the price of a pattern. Calls and tokens count only the target's own model calls, not the grader's.
  • A handful of questions is a smoke test, not a benchmark: differences of a few points are noise. Add questions to app/exercises/eval/datasets/ to get real signal.
