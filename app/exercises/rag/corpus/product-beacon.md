# Product: Beacon

## Overview
Beacon is Tidewell's hosted observability service: metrics, logs and traces
in one place, with alerting. It launched in 2021 and runs in two regions,
EU (Frankfurt) and US (Virginia).

## Ownership
Beacon's infrastructure, ingestion pipeline and on-call are owned by the
Platform team.

## Alerting engine
Beacon's alerting engine is known internally by the codename Kestrel. Kestrel
evaluates alert rules every 30 seconds and routes notifications to email,
Slack and the Klaxon paging tool.

## Plans
Beacon is sold per seat on a monthly basis in two plans, Team and Enterprise.
See the current pricing documents for prices.
