# Benefits Summary

## Time off
25 days of paid time off per year, plus local public holidays.

## Parental leave
20 weeks of fully paid parental leave for all parents, usable within the
first year after birth or adoption.

## Learning budget
Each employee has a learning budget of $1,500 per year for courses, books and
conferences. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Equipment
New employees choose a laptop; laptops are refreshed every 3 years.
