# Information Security Policy (summary)

## Accounts
Multi-factor authentication is required on every company account. Passwords
are stored in the company password manager.

## Laptops
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and automatic updates. Report a
lost or stolen laptop to the Security team within 24 hours.

## Certificates and keys
TLS certificates must be rotated at least every 90 days. Since June 2025,
certificate renewal is automated for all production systems.

## Data handling
Customer data must never be copied to personal devices or personal cloud
storage.
