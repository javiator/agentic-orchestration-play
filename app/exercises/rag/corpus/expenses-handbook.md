# Expenses Handbook

## Submitting expenses
Submit every expense with its receipt within 30 days. Expenses submitted
later than 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Approval thresholds
- Up to $2,000: approved by your manager.
- From $2,000 up to $10,000: approved by your department head.
- Over $10,000: must be approved by the Chief Financial Officer.

## Tier-1 cities
For hotel caps and per diems, these are tier-1 cities: London, New York,
San Francisco, Tokyo, Zurich and Singapore.

## Corporate cards
Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel more than four times a
year. Personal purchases on a corporate card are not allowed.
