# Product Roadmap 2025

## Harbor 3.0
General availability planned for Q3 2025. Headline feature: fully
air-gapped installation, with no outbound network access required.

## Beacon
Kestrel 2, a new version of the alerting engine with anomaly detection, is
planned for Q4 2025.

## Not planned
A mobile app is not on the 2025 roadmap.
