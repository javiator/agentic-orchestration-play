# Company Overview

Tidewell Labs is a fictional software company used as the document set for
this exercise. Nothing here describes a real organization.

## Who we are
The company was founded in 2019 and builds observability software for
engineering teams. The company has about 210 employees.

## Products
We sell two products: Beacon, a hosted observability service, and Harbor, a
self-hosted edition of Beacon for regulated industries such as banking and
healthcare.

## Locations
Headquarters are in Lisbon, with additional offices in Toronto and Singapore.
See the offices document for details on each site.
