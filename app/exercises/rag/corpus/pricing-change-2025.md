# Announcement: 2025 Price Change

## What changes
Effective 1 March 2025, Beacon list prices increase:
- Team plan: from $49 to $59 per seat per month
- Enterprise plan: from $89 to $99 per seat per month

## What does not change
Harbor license prices are unchanged in 2025. The 10% annual-prepayment
discount for Beacon remains.

## Existing customers
Customers on annual contracts keep their current price until their next
renewal date; the new price applies from renewal.
