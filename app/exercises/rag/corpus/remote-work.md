# Remote and Hybrid Work

## Hybrid schedule
Employees attached to an office work there at least two days a week, usually
Tuesday and Thursday.

## Working from another country
Employees may work from abroad for up to 30 days per calendar year. Requests
must be approved in advance by the People team, for tax and insurance
reasons.

## Fully remote roles
A small number of roles are fully remote; this is stated in the job offer.
