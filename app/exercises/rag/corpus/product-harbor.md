# Product: Harbor

## Overview
Harbor is the self-hosted edition of Beacon. Customers run it in their own
data centers or private cloud, which matters for banks, insurers and hospitals
that cannot send telemetry to a third party.

## Ownership
Harbor is built by the Enterprise Engineering team.

## Licensing
Harbor is licensed annually rather than per seat. See the pricing documents.

## Next major release
Harbor 3.0 is planned for 2025; see the 2025 roadmap for its scope and timing.
