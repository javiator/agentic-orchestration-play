# IT Help FAQ

## How do I get help?
Post in the #it-help channel or email the IT helpdesk. The helpdesk is
staffed 08:00–18:00 Lisbon time on weekdays.

## Can I install my own software?
Yes, from the approved software catalog. Anything else needs a Security team
review first.

## What about my own phone?
Personal phones can access email and chat after enrolling in device
management.
