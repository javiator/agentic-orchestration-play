# Platform On-Call Guide

## Rotation
The Platform team runs a weekly on-call rotation. Handoff happens every
Monday at 10:00 UTC.

## Paging
Pages are sent through Klaxon, the company paging tool. Acknowledge a page
within 5 minutes; escalate to the secondary on-call after 15 minutes.

## Compensation
Engineers on call receive a stipend of $300 per week on call.
