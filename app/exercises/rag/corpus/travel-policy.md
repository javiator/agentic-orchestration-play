# Travel Policy

## Booking
All business travel is booked through TripDesk, the company travel tool.
Trips must be approved by your manager before booking.

## Flights
Economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for
flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class is not reimbursed.

## Hotels
The standard nightly hotel cap is $220. In tier-1 cities the cap is raised to
$300 per night. The list of tier-1 cities is maintained in the expenses
handbook.

## Per diem
Meals are covered by a per diem of $75 per day while travelling.
